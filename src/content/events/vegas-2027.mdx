## Info

Join us for the **Second Annual Las Vegas Rally in the Desert** — April 14–19, 2027 at KOA at Sam's Town Casino and Hotel. Over 150+ coaches attended the 2026 rally, and we're going bigger in 2027. KOA has reserved the **entire South Campground** for this event.

**No registration fees for the event itself. Campground fees are paid directly to KOA. All are welcome, including expedition trailers.**

## Rates

- **$256.68** total for all five days
- $43.72/night weekdays · $58.70/night weekends
- Pull-through sites add $5 (weekday) / $10 (weekend)

Book early for best site selection.

## Site Map

- **Group Mod Tour sites:** #409–425 and #444–458
- **Storyteller Van sites:** #466–410 and #497–499
- **Vendor sites:** #225–245 and #428–436
- **Sites #216–219 and #221** are premium large sites with a dog run — inquire with KOA for pricing

- Group Mod Tours · Seminars · Hands-on demonstrations & installs

- Tour de Summerlin Red Rock Park bike ride
- Hoover Dam group bike ride departing from the KOA

- Live music and desert vibes
