Registration for the "Fall in Forest City" meetup is now open!

This informal gathering, sponsored by the Winnie Bs, invites Winnebago Class B and Compact Coach owners to join us. Arrival is on Tuesday, October 6, with departure on Saturday, October 10.

We'll be camping on the Winnebago Rally Grounds in Forest City, IA. Electric hookups will be available, along with water access at the end of each row and use of the shower house. A free dump station is available at Pammel Park about a mile away. We also plan to set up a tent where we can gather during the day and evening.

Winnebago has graciously provided the rally grounds at no cost to our group. Factory service is available, but appointments must be scheduled in advance. We are also hoping to offer a factory tour, depending on production schedules. The Winnie Bs Annual Meeting will take place during the meetup.

If you plan to attend, please register using the link above. This will help us estimate attendance, plan accordingly, and keep you informed as the event approaches.
