## Join us

Last year was incredible: 110 EKKOs, 200+ attendees, and a schedule packed with energy, activities, and a full day of vendor presentations. It was our biggest rally yet.

This year, we’re dialing in a different vibe — more relaxed, more social, more time to breathe. Expect a handful of great vendors, a few curated talks and activities, and plenty of space to unwind, connect, and soak in the desert magic.

We welcome all EKKO owners both new and returning! Your presence helps shape the experience, strengthens the community, and creates the kind of memories that keep people coming year after year.

**Registration opens July 1st. It’s $100 for the first adult and $50 for additional adults.** Spots usually fill up pretty fast, so signing up early is a good idea.

## 🙋 Looking for Activity Managers

We're looking for volunteers to organize and oversee activities this year:

- **Hiking Manager** — work with volunteer Hike Leaders to plan hikes for Saturday, Sunday, and Monday, all within Catalina State Park
- **Biking Manager** — work with volunteer Bike Leaders to plan rides for Saturday, Sunday, and Monday on the Chuck Huckleberry Loop, accessible from the park
- **Games Manager** — work with volunteer Game Leaders to plan lawn games, a cornhole contest, a scavenger hunt, a relay race, table games, and other fun activities

We're focusing less on vendors and more on outdoor activities and games this time around, and we'd love your help pulling it off. If organizing hiking, biking, or games sounds like a fun challenge, email us at [TucsonEKKORally@gmail.com](mailto:TucsonEKKORally@gmail.com).

### We hope to see you in November!
