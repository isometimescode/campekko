## The 6th Annual Tampa EKKO Rally

Registration for the 6th Annual Tampa EKKO Rally is now live! The rally is a beloved annual tradition that coincides with the Tampa RV Supershow. We're keeping the tradition alive, coming off a great first year hosting in January 2026, and looking to make it even better this time around — all while supporting Forgotten Angels, which provides resources to foster children who have aged out of the system.

We'll spend three days and nights dry camping on their beautiful grass campus overlooking a pond. As in years past, there will be hosted meals, speakers on relevant topics, mod tours, fun and games, and free time to attend the RV show. These rallies are a great way to make new friends and bond over all things Ekko and RV life in general.

## How to Attend

1. Fill out the [registration form](https://forms.gle/YYJcWP5VE5KPu1q96)
2. Fill out the [waiver form](https://forms.gle/MwrVxT1sBmmH86RN9)
3. Make your payment — see the registration form for details

The registration form has a section asking for volunteers — let us know if you'd be willing to assist in any way. Thank you so much!

## Giving Back

Proceeds support [Forgotten Angels](https://forgottenangelsflorida.org/), the host venue and nonprofit organization.
